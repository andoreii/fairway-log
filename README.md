# Fairway Log

Fairway Log is a small personal project for tracking my golf rounds hole by hole.

Mostly just a way to stop guessing about which parts of my game are getting better and what is actually costing me strokes.

## What it tracks

- Score and par by hole
- Putts and estimated first-putt distance
- Tee and approach accuracy
- Penalties
- Tee and approach clubs
- Course, tee, hole, and yardage

GIR and FIR will be worked out from the round data.
